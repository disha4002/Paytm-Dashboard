## Paytm-Dashboard
Developed an interactive Paytm Transaction Analysis Dashboard using Power BI to analyze transaction trends, revenue performance, customer behavior, and payment activity. 
<br>
The project involved data cleaning and transformation, data modeling, and the creation of interactive visualizations and DAX measures to generate actionable insights.
<br>
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of total transactions, transaction value, revenue, transaction trends, payment methods, customer segments, and geographic performance. Interactive slicers and filters allow users to drill down into specific time periods, transaction categories, and customer segments, making it easier to identify trends and performance patterns.

## Business questions it answers
What age group of customers use online banking the most?
<br>
What is the total number of paytm transcations?
<br>
Distinguishing the number of successful and failed transactions.

## Tools Used
Power BI desktop, power query, data transformation, data modelling and DAX measures.

## Overview
The analysis shows that Paytm's transaction activity is relatively consistent throughout the year, with June recording the highest monthly volume. Weekdays contribute significantly more transactions than weekends, while the Loans service is the dominant contributor to overall transaction volume. The user base is relatively balanced across major age groups, and successful transactions account for the vast majority of activity, indicating strong transaction completion performance.

## Key Insights 
High transaction volume:
The dashboard records approximately 3.47 billion total transactions across 108K users, indicating a very large transaction base.
<br>
Monthly transaction trend:
Transaction activity remains relatively stable around 280–300M per month.
<br>
June is the strongest month, reaching approximately 300M transactions.
<br>
February records one of the lowest levels at around 279M, followed by a recovery in March.
<br>
Weekend vs weekday activity:
Weekdays account for approximately 71.58% of transactions.
<br>
Weekends contribute around 28.42%, showing that transaction activity is considerably higher during weekdays.
<br>
Services drive different levels of activity:
Loans dominate transaction activity by a significant margin, with approximately 2.53B transactions.
<br>
Insurance follows at around 512.9M, while Money Transfer and Recharge contribute substantially less.
<br>
This indicates that the Loans category is the primary driver of transaction volume in the dataset.
<br>
User demographics:
The dashboard shows a fairly balanced distribution among the major age segments.
<br>
Gen X and Millennials each represent roughly 37% of users, while Gen Z and Boomers account for the remaining share.
<br>
Transaction status:
Successful transactions make up the overwhelming majority across the months.
<br>
Failed, insufficient-amount, server-error and wrong-PIN transactions represent comparatively small portions of overall activity.
<br>
Top users by transaction activity:
Michael Smith has the highest transaction count among the displayed users, at approximately 1.82M transactions.
<br>
Jennifer Williams follows with around 1.57M, while the remaining top users are clustered around 1.2–1.3M.
